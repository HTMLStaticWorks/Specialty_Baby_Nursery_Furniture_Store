Professional Assembly

Nursery furniture requires precise assembly to guarantee absolute safety. Our trained technicians know every piece inside and out.

  • Expert assembly: Factory-trained professionals ensuring flawless construction.
  • Safe setup: Verification of structural integrity and safety hardware installation.
  • Placement assistance: We'll place the furniture exactly where you want it in the room.
  • Packaging removal: All boxes, plastic, and debris are removed and recycled for you.
  • Final inspection: A thorough walkthrough to ensure everything meets our standards.
