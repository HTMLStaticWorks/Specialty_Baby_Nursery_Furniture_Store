Harvested from certified sustainable American forests. Kiln-dried to 6-8% moisture content to prevent warping, cracking, or joint loosening across decades.

Stain-Resistant Performance Bouclé

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certified. Free of fluorochemicals, PFAS, and chemical fire retardants. Liquid spills bead on the surface for effortless spot cleaning.

99% UV Museum-Grade Acrylic

Lightweight and shatterproof for nursery safety. Blocks 99% of damaging UV rays to prevent baby footprints, ultrasound photos, and milestone art from yellowing or fading.
