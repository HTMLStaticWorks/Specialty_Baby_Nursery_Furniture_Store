Our Story

Born From a Mother’s Search For Truly Pure Furniture

Nestora began when our founders, Elena and Julian Wright, prepared for their first child. Searching for a convertible crib, they encountered a frustrating reality: mainstream furniture was often treated with harsh chemical finishes, synthetic adhesives, and thin composite boards disguised with decorative veneers.

With backgrounds in architectural interior design and traditional fine woodworking, they partnered with veteran furniture artisans in the Appalachian valley to craft their own crib from sustainably harvested solid oak, sealed only with natural food-grade beeswax.

Today, Nestora has grown into a premier specialty nursery atelier. Every crib, glider, dresser, and custom framed keepsake we produce continues that same founding vow: nothing enters your baby’s sanctuary that we wouldn’t place in our own home.

Specialized Expertise

Why We Specialize Exclusively In Nursery Furniture

General home furniture is built for adults who know how to avoid sharp edges and pinch hazards. Nursery furniture demands an entirely separate tier of engineering, testing, and care:

Precision Slat Clearance & Anti-Entrapment

Our crib slats are calibrated to 2-1/8 inches of uniform clearance, preventing head and limb entrapment according to the strictest pediatric guidelines.

Teething-Safe Organic Edges

Infants explore with their mouths. Our top rails are shaped with soft bevels and finished with chew-safe, 100% non-toxic food-grade mineral finishes.

Integrated Anti-Tip Steel Interlocks

Every dresser features internal drawer interlocking mechanisms that prevent more than one drawer from opening at once, paired with ASTM F2057 heavy-duty safety wall anchoring.

Archival Nursery Memory Framing

Unlike cheap decorative frames with shatterable plate glass, our nursery art & memory frames use shatterproof museum-grade acrylic and 99% UV-blocking preservation.
